## The Take Over

- In a world already dominated by robots, the development of new models began. These new robots are smarter, stronger, and powered by self-sustaining energy; upon realizing their superiority, they begin to attack the obsolete robots.

- You, in control of theses new robots,  have to find all source of energy to olds robots and take over the world.
